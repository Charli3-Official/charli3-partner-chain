# Commercial Licensing

Charli3 Oracles (C3 Oracle International Ltd.) offers commercial licenses
for organizations that wish to:

- Host or operate their own partner-chain
- Offer oracle networks as a managed or hosted service
- Embed Charli3 oracle infrastructure in a commercial platform
- Use the core system in ways restricted by the Business Source License

Commercial licenses are offered on a case-by-case basis and tailored to
the specific deployment and business model.

To inquire about a commercial license, contact: **sales@charli3.io**

Please include:

- Company name
- Intended use
- Deployment model (hosted, embedded, internal)
- Expected scale (chains, nodes, feeds, revenue, etc.)
